## The problem with how PMs use AI

Most people use AI the way they use a search engine — one-off prompts, no memory, no structure. You get decent output for simple tasks, but the moment you're working on something nuanced — a PRD, a strategy doc, a feature brief — the output is generic, off-brand, and requires heavy editing.

The AI doesn't know who you are. It doesn't know your product. It doesn't know your constraints. Every conversation starts from zero.

## The fix: a context file

A `context.md` is a structured markdown file that carries everything an AI needs to work well with you — your role, your product, your team, your writing style, your current priorities, your constraints. You maintain it. You paste it (or reference it) at the start of every session.

The difference in output quality is significant. Instead of generic PM-speak, you get responses that sound like you wrote them — specific, opinionated, and grounded in your actual context.
